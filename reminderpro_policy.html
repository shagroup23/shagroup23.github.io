Reminder Pro Privacy Policy

Stay organized. Get things done.

Effective date: September 25, 2026

1. Who we are

Reminder Pro is provided by SHA Group 23 (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy explains how Reminder Pro handles information when you use the Android application.

2. Information the app handles

  • Reminder information: names, dates, times, repeat days, enabled status, alarm and notification choices, and related settings that you enter.
  • Voice notes: if you grant microphone permission and record a voice note, the recording is saved in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Audio files: if you choose an audio file, Reminder Pro copies it into the app’s private storage so it can be used with your reminder.
  • Device permissions: notification, exact-alarm, microphone, vibration, wake-lock, and boot/time-change permissions are used only to provide the reminder features you request.

Reminder data, voice notes, and copied audio files are stored locally on your device. Reminder Pro does not provide user accounts or its own cloud storage, and we do not sell your reminder content.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Reminder Pro uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner advertisements. Google and its partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction information according to their policies and your device or consent choices.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s information about how it uses data from apps.

4. How information is used

We use locally stored information to create, edit, schedule, enable, disable, and deliver reminders, notifications, alarms, voice notes, and audio playback. Advertising information is used by Google to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share your reminder names, schedules, voice recordings, or selected audio files with our own servers. Information may be processed by Google Mobile Ads as necessary to serve advertisements. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can refuse optional permissions, disable notifications or alarms, delete reminders, and remove attached recordings or audio through the app. You can clear the app’s storage or uninstall Reminder Pro to remove locally stored app data. You can manage Android advertising privacy and reset advertising identifiers in your device settings. Ad choices may also be available through the ad controls shown by Google.

7. Children’s privacy

Reminder Pro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

8. Security and retention

Local app data is protected by Android’s app storage permissions. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies.

9. Disclaimer and limitation of liability

Reminder Pro is provided on an “as is” and “as available” basis. Reminder delivery may be affected by device settings, battery optimization, power-saving modes, operating-system restrictions, notification permissions, Do Not Disturb, network availability, third-party services, or other circumstances outside our control.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, SHA Group 23 is not liable for missed, delayed, duplicated, or otherwise unsuccessful reminders, alarms, notifications, audio playback, data loss, or indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages arising from or related to use of the app. Nothing in this policy excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, applicable law, or third-party services change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact SHA Group 23 through the SHA Group 23 GitHub profile.
